eDIS stands for Electronic Delivery Instruction Slip. It is a facility introduced by CDSL that allows you to sell shares if you have not submitted your Power of Attorney (POA) to your broking company like Flattrade. It is to be noted that you can only sell the shares you are holding in your linked Demat account. Let us take a look at using the e-DIS facility.

## Steps to sell shares through e-DIS when you already have a TPIN
1. Login to you account using your credentials.2. Click “Holdings”. “E-DIS” button will appear. Press the “E-DIS” button. Next screen will appear3. Press “Generate EDIS”4. A new screen will appear with your holding shares and you can select the shares you wish to sell. 5. Enter the TPIN to to give authorization. 6. You will get an OTP to confirm your action. 7. Now you will be able to sell your shares.

## Steps to sell shares through e-DIS when you do not have a TPIN
The TPIN can be created randomly or according to your choice
A) Creating TPIN randomly1. Login to you account using your credentials.2. Click “Holdings”. “E-DIS” button will appear.3. Press the “E-DIS” button. You will taken to the next screen.4. Here, select “Generate random TPIN”. Now, you will receive the random TPIN through email and SMS. Now enter the random TPIN. Finally, you will receive an OTP to confirm you action. 5. Now you can sell you shares using the randomly generated TPIN. This random TPIN can be used on subsequent days also as it is permanent. You do not have to create random TPIN everyday.
B) Creating TPIN according to your choice1. Login to you account using your credentials.2. Click “Holdings”. “E-DIS” button will appear.3. Press the “E-DIS” button. You will be taken to the next screen.4. Press “Create TPIN of you choice”. Next screen will appear.5. Enter your BO ID and PAN. Now press “Next” button. You will receive an OTP to your registered email id and mobile number.6. Enter the OTP and press “Next”7. Now enter a TPIN of your choice under “New TPIN” and repeat it under “Confirm TPIN”. Press “Submit” to confirm. Now TPIN would be created successfully.8. Now you can sell you shares using the TPIN created as per your choice. This TPIN is also permanent.
Note: You must generate E-DIS everyday if you wish to sell you shares.
