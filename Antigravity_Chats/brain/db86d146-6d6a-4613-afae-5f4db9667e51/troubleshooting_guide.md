# How to Stop Unwanted & 18+ Ads on Your Phone

If you are receiving intrusive ads (especially 18+ ones), it is usually due to one of three things: **browser notification hijack**, **adware (malicious apps)**, or **personalized ad tracking**.

Follow these steps in order to stop them:

---

## 1. Block Ads via Private DNS (Best Method)
This hides ads across your entire phone (apps and browser) without installing anything.
*   **Android**: Settings > Network & Internet > Private DNS > Select 'Private DNS provider hostname' > Type `dns.adguard.com` > Save.
*   **iOS**: Settings > Wi-Fi > Tap the 'i' next to your network > Configure DNS > Manual > Add Server `94.140.14.14`.

---

## 2. Stop Browser Notification Ads
Rogue websites often "hijack" your notifications to show 18+ ads.
*   **Chrome (Android)**: Open Chrome > Menu (3 dots) > Settings > Site Settings > Notifications.
*   **Action**: Look for any suspicious websites in the "Allowed" list and select **Remove** or **Block**.
*   **Safari (iOS)**: Settings > Notifications > Safari > Ensure suspicious sites are turned off.

---

## 3. Find and Delete Rogue Apps
Some "cleaner," "battery saver," or "free game" apps run in the background and show ads.
*   **Go to**: Settings > Apps > See all apps.
*   **Look for**:
    *   Apps with **no icon** or **no name**.
    *   Apps you don't remember installing.
    *   Recently installed apps from unofficial sources.
*   **Action**: Uninstall anything suspicious.
*   **Check "Display over other apps"**: Settings > Apps > Special app access > Display over other apps. Disable anything that shouldn't be showing pop-ups.

---

## 4. Reset Advertising ID
This clears your browsing profile that ad networks use to target you.
*   **Android**: Settings > Google > Ads > **Delete advertising ID**.
*   **iOS**: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking > Toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

---

## 5. Enable Play Protect
*   Open **Google Play Store**.
*   Tap your profile icon > **Play Protect** > **Scan**.
*   This will check for known malicious apps that serve ads.

> [!TIP]
> **Prevention**: Avoid clicking "Allow" on any website that asks to show notifications unless you absolutely trust it. Also, avoid downloading "modded" APKs from third-party sites, as these often contain adware.
