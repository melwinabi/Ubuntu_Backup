A homeowner wants access to his residence to be controlled by an automatic gate equipped with a dual direction (opening and closure) motor.

Opening: Whether the gate is closed or in an intermediate position, the remote control signal causes the full opening of the gate. During the opening process, any new action on the remote control stops or restarts the motor.

As soon as the gate is fully open, a 4-second time delay delays its closure. Closure: During the closing process, if the remote control is activated or if the sensor detects a passage, the gate is opened. As long as the sensor is activated, (vehicle stopped in the passage way for example), the gate remains fully open.
