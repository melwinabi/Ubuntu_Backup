A high school wants to control the daily bell and alarm system using the same device. The bell rings from Monday to Friday for one minute except during the holidays. The "alarm" mode is active during the holidays, on weekends, and at night Monday through Friday.

The alert is given for 1 minute by an audio signal alternating 2 s ON, 1 s OFF, and by an indicator light activated by a motion detector. It must be possible to reset the alarm.
